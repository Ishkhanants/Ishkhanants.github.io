Dominoes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Dominoes (package com.smartin.dominoes) handles information.

Summary

Dominoes does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your settings (sound, theme, language, animation speed, default difficulty and target score);
  • game statistics (games played, wins and best round score);
  • the match in progress, so you can continue it later.

This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. You can also reset your statistics at any time from the Statistics screen.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No third-party services that receive data.

Opponents in the game are computer players; their usernames are generated randomly on your device and do not belong to real people.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example online accounts), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
